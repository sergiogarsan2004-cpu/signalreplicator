Reader app for MQL5 Market buyers

The Expert Advisor you bought or rented on the MQL5 Market works together with this free app. The app reads your Telegram channels on your PC and passes each signal to the EA, which opens the order in MetaTrader 5.

Download for Windows · 25 MB The EA on the MQL5 Market

Windows only, on the same PC or Windows VPS as MetaTrader 5. The app only works with the EA from the MQL5 Market, so you don't need any licence key.

Setup in six steps

  1. In MetaTrader 5, open Navigator › Expert Advisors › Market › Telegram To MT5 Signal Copier SignalReplicator. Drag it onto any chart (for example XAUUSD M1), turn on Algo Trading and keep that chart open.
  2. Unzip the file you downloaded and open SignalReplicator_MQL5.exe. If Windows SmartScreen shows a warning, choose More info › Run anyway.
  3. Settings › Account: enter the phone number of your Telegram account with the country code, press Send code to Telegram and type the code you receive in Telegram (plus your two-step password if you use one). The session is stored only on your PC.
  4. Data folder: press Detect so the app finds your MetaTrader 5.
  5. Group 1: type the channel name exactly as it appears in Telegram, the default symbol and your lot size. Every safety setting is off until you turn it on.
  6. Press Save settings, then Start bot. The activity log shows Connected and listening.

If the log says This edition only works with the EA bought or rented on the MQL5 Market, the EA is not running on a chart: go back to step 1. For any question, write in the Comments tab of the product on the MQL5 Market.
