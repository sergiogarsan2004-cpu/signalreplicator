Questions people actually ask

Do I have to give anyone my broker password?

No. You create your own MetaApi account and enter your broker details there, in your name. We connect through MetaApi with a token you can revoke at any time. We never hold your broker credentials, and we never touch your funds.

What about my Telegram account?

You log in with your own Telegram account and pick which channels to copy. Our access is read-only by design: we read the messages in the channels you choose. We don't post, we don't message anyone, and we don't touch your contacts.

What if my channel posts signals in an odd format?

The parser handles the usual formats in Spanish, English, French, German and Portuguese, including partial closes, stop-loss moves and edited messages. If your channel posts something it can't read, send it to us — that's how the parser gets better.

Do I have to leave my computer on?

Not with the Cloud. It runs on our servers, so you can close the laptop and go away. The desktop version does need your machine on with MetaTrader 5 open.

Can I pause it from my phone?

Yes. With Telegram commands you can pause, resume, see your open trades and change your lot size from anywhere.

Do I need a card to try it?

No. The 7-day trial doesn't ask for one.

What happens if your server goes down?

We publish our uptime and incidents at status.signalreplicator.com, so you don't have to take our word for it.
