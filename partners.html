The problem that eats your time

You post a good entry. Half your channel was asleep, at work, or on a train. They see it twenty minutes later, get in at a worse price, and when the trade doesn't work out they message you about it.

You did your job properly. They didn't get there in time. But you're the one who takes the complaint — and sometimes the review.

What SignalReplicator does

It reads your Telegram channel and places the trade on the member's own MetaTrader 5: entry, stop loss, every take profit, and partial closes as they hit.

What matters to you:

  • Nothing about your channel changes. You post the way you always have, in your own format. You install nothing, you give us no access, we don't touch your group.
  • It decides nothing. Your call is your call. We only execute it.
  • Each member sets their own risk. Their lot size, their limits, their broker. You're not responsible for how anyone configures it.
  • We don't compete with you. We don't sell signals and we don't run a channel. Without people like you we have no product.

And it's free for you, permanently

Whether you bring one member or none at all, you never pay. Not a two-week trial, not a welcome discount: free for as long as you run the channel.
